FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about working with Cyberville.

Where is Cyberville located?

Cyberville is a software development company based in Juba, South Sudan, working with clients across South Sudan and East Africa. Call or WhatsApp +211 92 273 4334 to reach us.

How much does a website or software cost in South Sudan?

Every project is different. A simple business website costs far less than a full pharmacy or hospital management system. Send us a short description of your project and we will reply with a free quote, usually within 24 hours.

Do you build pharmacy management systems?

Yes. We built the system that runs Global Pharmacy, including point of sale, inventory and expiry tracking, and daily sales reports. We can build the same kind of system for your pharmacy.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

Most business websites are ready within 1 to 3 weeks. Larger systems like hospital or accounting software take longer because we design them around how your business actually works.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We stay with you after launch and remain available for updates, fixes and training whenever you need us.
